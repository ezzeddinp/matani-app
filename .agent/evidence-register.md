## How to read this document

This register is the single canonical home for externally sourced facts. Read it this way:

- **Claims are cited, not restated.** Every EVID-* and OBS-* entry states one atomic fact once, here. Other documents in this repo reference the ID (e.g. "the 5-day nominal revisit — EVID-006") and add a short role-specific gloss; they must never re-copy the canonical wording or the underlying number. If you find a statistic loose in another document, it belongs here first.
- **A new external claim is added here before it appears anywhere else.** If you want to write "Sentinel-2 does X" or "Indonesian farms are Y" in the research protocol, product brief, or architecture doc, add the EVID entry first, verify its source, then cite the ID. No forward references to claims that do not yet exist in this register.
- **HTTP 200 is not evidence.** A reachable endpoint, a returned JSON body, or a rendered page proves the service responded — not that a specific fact is true. Evidence means the named source's own content, at the named section/table/page, states the claim. The "Checked" date is filled only when the source content was actually read on that date, not when a URL merely resolved.
- **The "Checked" column is a date, and a promise.** Perishable claims (quotas, pricing, coverage) carry a date because they expire. Recheck them before any decision that depends on them; a stale date is a warning, not a guarantee.
- **Limitation / permitted wording is binding.** The right-hand column states what the entry does and does not license you to say, including geographic or contextual scope. Do not generalize a single-location observation or a pan-tropical statistic into a nationwide claim.
- **Provenance markers.** External claims are `EVID-*`; dated observations are `OBS-*`. Repository facts (`CODE-*` / `file:line`), product decisions (`DEC-*`), and unvalidated thresholds (`HYP-*`) live in their canonical documents per `AGENTS.md`, not here.

## Adding new evidence

Before any new external fact appears anywhere in this repository, add it here first, under these rules:

1. **One atomic claim per entry.** If a sentence contains two facts, it is two entries. Do not bundle a definition with a limitation, or a quota with an auth mechanism.
2. **Primary source, named precisely.** Prefer the authoritative publisher: ESA/Copernicus documentation, a peer-reviewed paper with DOI, official provider documentation, or BPS/FAO. Name the exact section, table, page, or property that supports the claim — a bare homepage URL is not enough.
3. **Checked date = the day you actually read the source content.** Fill it only if you verified the claim against the source's own words on that date. HTTP 200, a resolving URL, or a returned body is not verification.
4. **If it cannot be verified, say so.** For bot-blocked PDFs, paywalls, or dead endpoints, either find a verifiable alternative primary source or mark the entry `Status: UNVERIFIED — needs manual check` with the reason. Never invent, estimate, or round a statistic into existence.
5. **State the limitation and permitted wording, including scope.** Record what the entry does and does not license, plus geographic/contextual applicability. A pan-tropical statistic is not an Indonesia claim; a single-location observation is not a national one; a nominal satellite spec is not a monitoring cadence.
6. **Mark perishable claims.** Quotas, pricing, and coverage carry a checked date and a "recheck before deployment" note — they are treated as expiring.
7. **Cite by ID elsewhere; never duplicate canonical wording.** Other documents reference the EVID-*/OBS-* ID with a short role-specific gloss. This register stays the one home for the fact.

Fewer, solid entries beat many shaky ones.
